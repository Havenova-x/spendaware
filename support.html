Frequently asked questions

Is SpendAware free?

Yes. You can download and use SpendAware for free on Windows and Android.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. The app only goes online briefly to check for announcements.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own phone or PC. We do not have an account system and cannot see your records.

How do I move my data to another device?

Open Settings, choose Backup, and save the .spendaware file. On the other device, open that file or choose Restore.

How do I add something by typing?

Open Quick add and type, for example: add expense: 1000, lunch. You can also use add income, add debt or add owe. The reason is optional.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install?

New apps without a paid certificate can trigger SmartScreen. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can verify the download with the SHA-256 code shown in the download section.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. SpendAware is available for Windows and Android.

Tips

  • Back up from Settings now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe.
  • If reminders stop on Android, check that notifications are still allowed for SpendAware.
  • On Windows, keep SpendAware running in the tray if you want reminders.
