Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

SpendAware is made by Havenova X. We designed it so your money records stay with you. This page explains what the app and this website do with information.

Your records stay on your device

The income, expenses, debts, names, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your phone or computer. We do not run accounts or servers that receive your records, and we cannot see them.

What the app connects to

The app works offline. It occasionally makes a small request to GitHub to read public files that hold announcements and banner messages. Like any internet request, GitHub can see your IP address. We do not send your records or any personal details in these requests.

Permissions (Android)

  • Notifications: to remind you about debts. This is optional and you choose whether to turn it on.
  • Internet: for the announcement check and to load ads on Android.
  • Files: when you back up or restore, you pick the file yourself using your phone’s file picker.

Backups and sharing

Backup files are saved where you choose. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe. If you share a backup, the person who receives it can read it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs, which are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Advertising (Android)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: small banners, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to earn coins. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads, and may use cookies or similar technology. See Google’s advertising policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone’s Google settings. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first. Your income, expense and debt records are never shared with advertisers.

Banners from Havenova X

The app may also show a banner or announcement loaded from a public file. It does not use your personal data.

Coins

Coins you earn by watching rewarded videos are stored only on your device.

Children

SpendAware does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or use the erase option, to remove your data from that device. Delete your backup files separately.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions? Use the support page or visit Havenovax.name.ng.
